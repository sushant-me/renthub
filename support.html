Support and FAQ

Android app

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK from the download section, then open it. Android may ask you to allow installs from this source. Allow it, finish the install, and you can turn the setting off again afterwards.

How do I check the download?

The download section lists the SHA-256 checksum for version 1.0.0. After downloading, compare the checksum shown by your file manager with the one on the page. They should match exactly.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and later. The app is 63.5 MB and installs directly, without a store account.

Using Rent Hub

Is Rent Hub free?

Browsing and standard listings are free. Optional featured placement and boosts are paid. There are no agency fees on a standard listing.

How do inspections work?

Open a listing and pick a 15-minute inspection slot. The owner or agent sees the booking in their dashboard. You can cancel from your inspections page if plans change.

Why does a listing say Synced from Facebook?

It started as a post in the public Landlords and Tenants Direct Facebook group. An admin approved it before it appeared in Rent Hub, and the original author and link are kept with the listing.

How do I report a listing?

Use the report button on the listing page. Reports go to an admin queue. Choose the closest reason and add a short note if it helps.

Can agents list?

Yes. Agents get an Agent badge and the same listing and enquiry tools. A subscription is not needed to publish a listing.

iPhone and iPad

Where is the iOS app?

The iPhone app is built from the same Flutter codebase as the Android app. Installing it on an iPhone needs an Apple Developer signing profile, so iOS is coming via TestFlight. The Android build is available now.

Web console

Do I need a separate web account?

No. The web console uses the same account as the app. Owners, agents and admins see different tools based on their role.

What is in the console?

Listings and lifecycle actions, the three-step create wizard, inspections, messages, and an admin area for Facebook sync, moderation, KYC and reports.

Account and privacy

How do I delete my account?

Contact support from the address in your account and ask for deletion. We will remove or anonymise your personal information where we can, and explain anything we need to keep for legal reasons.

Who can see my phone number?

Other users cannot see it in a listing or a chat. Rent Hub uses in-app messaging so you can deal with the owner without publishing a phone number.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

The privacy policy explains what is collected and why. The terms of use cover the marketplace rules.

Safety

  • Inspect the property before sending any bond or holding deposit.
  • Keep the conversation on Rent Hub so there is a record.
  • Report a listing if the price, photos or address do not look right.
  • Do not share account details with anyone.
