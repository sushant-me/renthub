1. What Rent Hub is

Rent Hub is a marketplace that lets private owners and agents list rental properties and lets renters find them. Rent Hub is not a real estate agent, is not a party to any lease, and does not provide legal, tenancy or financial advice. The agreement for a rental is between the renter and the owner or agent.

  • Give accurate details and keep your password private.
  • Choose the right role: renter, private owner or agent.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or harms other users.

3. Listings

  • You must own the property or be authorised to list it.
  • The rent, bond, address and description must be accurate and not misleading.
  • Use up to six photos. Only upload photos you have the right to use.
  • Do not include discriminatory or unlawful wording. Listings are scanned under New South Wales Fair Trading rules and may be blocked before publishing.
  • We may remove a listing after a report is upheld, or when it breaks these terms.

4. Agents

Agents can publish listings with an Agent badge without a subscription. Paid plans and listing boosts are optional and are charged at the price shown in the app or web console at the time of purchase.

5. Facebook group posts

Public posts from the Landlords and Tenants Direct Facebook group may be imported into a review queue. An admin checks each post before it is published. Published posts keep the original author name and a link to the original post. If you want a post removed, contact support.

6. Free and paid features

Browsing and standard listing are free. Optional featured placement and boosts are paid features and are not required to use Rent Hub. Prices are shown before you pay. Payments are handled by our payment provider.

7. Inspections and messaging

  • Keep conversations on Rent Hub so there is a record if something goes wrong.
  • Do not use messaging for spam, harassment, or attempts to defraud another person.
  • Inspection slots are 15 minutes unless the owner or agent says otherwise.
  • Never send a bond or holding deposit before you have inspected a property.

8. Prohibited conduct
